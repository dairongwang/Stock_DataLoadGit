A schedule loads one day of every stock of an exchange at its time (server time) on each day the exchange trades (weekends and holidays are skipped), once per day - if the dashboard was not running at that time, the run follows as soon as it runs again that day. It is an ordinary bulk load: the daily limits apply (3 calls per exchange and trading day, 3 whole-market loads a day, 5 loads per stock), and its progress shows under Current load. A refused or failed run is not repeated that day.
