Active tables staging → daily → weekly / monthly

The staging table holds files as delivered. Promote daily validates the staged rows (date, numbers, prices > 0, high ≥ low, open / close within low..high, whole-number volume, market open) and writes one typed row per stock and day into {{ settings.daily_table }} (UNIQUE ticker + day). Promote weekly / monthly aggregate the daily table into one row per stock and ISO week / month (open of the first day, highest high, lowest low, close of the last day, summed volume). Rejected rows are listed under data errors; correct them and promote again.
