Stock symbols Stock_Symbol - market, first and last trade date per symbol

Every symbol the loader sees is registered here with the market of its file and the range of dates loaded (first seen .. last seen). The first trade date (and, for delisted stocks, the last trade date) is a fact about the listing you maintain - by hand or by importing a CSV. Data dated before the first trade date (or after the last) is invalid: it is flagged at load time (before_first_trade_date / after_last_trade_date under data errors) and rejected by the promotion. The missing-market-day alert is only raised inside a symbol's trading window - a symbol without a first trade date is not checked for missing days.
